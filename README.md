## What you can do

**🚀 Simulator**
- Set up a throw by **speed and angle**, by its **x and y components**, or by a **force** and how long it acts
- Change the **launch height**, **ground slope** and **sideways aim**, then watch it fly in **2D or 3D** with play, pause, ±5 s skips and 0.5×–5× playback
- Throw on **Earth, the Moon, Mars, Jupiter** or a world you design, with a baseball, golf ball, football, cannonball, ping-pong ball, feather or your own object
- Everything starts in an **ideal world**. Turn on **air resistance**, **wind** (set its direction on a compass) and **bouncing** to see what real air does
- **Pin paths** to compare throws, and find the **angle that goes farthest** for any setup
- Graphs of **height, speed and energy**, plus a **step-by-step maths** walkthrough using your own numbers
- **Ready-made throws** like Galileo's 45°, a cliff throw and the hammer-and-feather drop, with tips that change as you experiment

**🌊 Relative Motion**
- **River & boat:** upstream and downstream trips, crossing in the least time, crossing by the shortest path, or any heading you choose, all animated with labelled velocity arrows
- **Rain & umbrella:** standing still, walking, or making the rain look vertical, seen from the pavement or through the walker's eyes, with an umbrella that tilts the right way

**🎮 Quiz & Games**
- An **adaptive quiz** across 8 topics, with fresh numbers every time, hints, a timed mode, XP and ranks, and your strongest and weakest areas
- **50 target challenges** in five tiers, from Beginner to Master, with walls, slopes, wind, cliffs and other planets. Earn up to three stars on each

**📏 Instruments:** the **vernier calliper, screw gauge, spherometer, metre rule and measuring cylinder**. Explore how each one reads, practise readings (with zero errors if you want a challenge), and learn how it works

**📜 History:** 40 moments, from Aristotle's "pushing air" and Galileo's parabola to Newton's cannonball, the metric system, Apollo golf on the Moon and Hawk-Eye. Many link straight to a throw you can try

**👥 Visitors:** see how many people have launched things on the site, and from which countries

Works on computers and phones.
